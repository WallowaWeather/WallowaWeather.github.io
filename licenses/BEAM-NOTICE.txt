BEAM (Blended Early-warning Alert Model)
Copyright 2026 Joshua Hughes

BEAM was created by artist Josh Hughes as a public-good project: the chance of lightning, hail, cloudbursts and strong
storm cores for the next 90 minutes, worked out on a phone from NOAA radar, GOES-West satellite and lightning data and
the HRRR model.
